## Title frames: how much each lifts views

vidIQ title study (4M+ videos from 2026). Each multiple compares videos using the frame with the average video.

| Frame | Multiple | Notes for the AI niche |
|---|---|---|
| Witness / "caught on camera" | >4x | Only works with real footage of the moment, such as a screen recording of the failure |
| Transformation ("X into Y") | >3x | "Turned 4 Terminals Into 1 Chat" |
| Insider ("nobody knows") | >2.5x | Needs a real fact the viewer can't get elsewhere |
| "Inside X" | 2.5x | Infra stories, e.g. "Inside Anthropic's Git Replacement" |
| Warning ("stop / don't / never") | ~2x | Make it about a decision ("before you switch"), not preaching |
| Story ("how X did Y") | ~2x | |
| Duration ("X days", overnight) | ~1.5x | Stack it with a test frame |
| Question-ending titles | 0.82x | Avoid |
| "I was wrong" confessions | <0.5x | Avoid |

## The AI niche: winners vs losers

Data: last 90 days. Winners are videos at ≥3x their channel's subscriber count with ≥100K views (n=95). Losers are videos at <0.3x on channels with 50K+ subscribers (n=157).

| Feature | Winners | Losers |
|---|---|---|
| Short verdict "[Name] is [word]." | median **5.1x** (n=24), against a 0.72x base | n/a |
| Negative / catch framing | 15% | 6% |
| vs / beat | 12% | 6% |
| "NEW" | 4% | 9% |
| "changes everything" / "game changer" | 1% | 3% |
| Median title length | 52 chars | 56 chars |

**Short verdicts:**
- Examples: Cole at 31x, Less Bitter at 27x, Minimunch at 1.39M views.
- They only work when the thumbnail proves the verdict.

**Coming second with a better angle beats coming first:**
- Projects: Riley on day 4 did 0.47x; Mansel on day 15 did 10x.
- Mods: a day-1 first look did 1.21x; RoboNuggets on day 6, with concrete uses, did 3.4x.

## Thumbnails

**Text:**
- 28% of breakout thumbnails have none. Most that do use 3 words or fewer.
- The text complements the title rather than repeating it. 13 of the 14 high-ratio niche thumbnails follow this.

**TV:** rows may show only the top half of a thumbnail, so the top half has to sell the video on its own.

**Faces:**
- 69% of breakouts show a face.
- Faceless winners in this niche use a 3D Claude-orange object or mascot, an output collage, or a before/after.

**Styles in vogue** (vidIQ, Aug 2026):
- inside-the-head metaphor
- simplified 3–6-image grid
- "leaked blueprint"
- monochrome / prestige book cover
- brutal split-screen
- overhead shot
- triangle of 3 headshots
- removing a mask
- red FOMO trend line

**Retire:**
- **Raw UI crops.** On recent Claude launches: Riley 0.47x, Nate 0.22x, AI Foundations 0.11x.
- **Dense diagrams.** A whiteboard map got 0.10x.
- **The coordinator → green-checks fan-out diagram.** It has already been used 7+ times.

## Platform changes since May 2026

| When | Change | What it means for packaging |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 2025 → now | Test & Compare: 3 variants, chosen on watch-time share. A control group sees only the default. Editing mid-test stops the test. Desktop Studio only. YouTube recommends testing older videos first. | Optimize for holding viewers, not for clicks |
| Mar 2026 | Test of replacing creator titles with AI summaries (Android, limited) | The thumbnail has to carry the concept alone |
| May–Sep 2026 | Ask YouTube (Gemini search) and custom Home feeds | Entity nouns still matter, but search is only 5–10% of traffic for most channels |
| Aug 2026 | The Inspiration tab is deprecated and replaced by Ask Studio | Never send anyone to the Inspiration tab |
| 2026-08-24 | Public views count from the first frame. The old definition is now "engaged views" in Studio, about 60% of public views. | Any baseline that spans 08-24 is inflated |
| 2026-09-01 | Creator Insider: "no metric on its own" decides. CTR and AVD fall as views rise. One viewer may see a video ~19 times before clicking. Desktop thumbnails got bigger. | Raw-CTR swap rules no longer hold |
| 2026-09-23 | Made on YouTube: Studio AI titles and thumbnails in the channel's style, dynamic thumbnails (up to 3, by audience segment), hook A/B testing of video cuts (soon), Studio auto-swap of thumbnails (later) | The testing toolkit is now 4 tools |
